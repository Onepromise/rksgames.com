About

HORROR THAT
STAYS WITH YOU

Rider Kick Studios is an independent game studio obsessed with one thing: horror that lingers long after you put the controller down.

Our first title, Room 303, is a first-person horror game set in the neon underbelly of Kabukicho, Tokyo — a love letter to J-horror, built room by room in Unreal Engine 5.

Our mission

NOT JUST GAMES —
EXPERIENCES THAT MATTER

Rider Kick Studios was born from a shared passion: to create new stories and experiences that resonate. We're a group of people who came together with a simple but powerful goal — to get back to what gaming is really about. We want to connect with players on a deeper level and craft narratives that don't just entertain, but inspire.

In a world where games can sometimes feel formulaic, we're driven by the desire to build something different. We believe the best games are the ones that stay with you long after you've put down the controller — the ones that make you think, feel, and see the world from a new angle.

That's why every project we undertake is designed to invoke a sense of mystery and adventure. Whether you're unraveling secrets, forging unexpected alliances, or discovering truths hidden in plain sight, our games are built to surprise and captivate.
