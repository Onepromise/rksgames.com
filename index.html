ROOM 303 / 三百三

Room 303 is watching.

Kabukicho, Tokyo. John came to Japan chasing inspiration for his next game. Instead he checked into a hotel that remembers every guest — and three of them never left.

Your phone is your only weapon. Its camera reveals what the naked eye can't see. Its flashlight keeps the dark at bay. And its messages aren't all from the living.

Five rooms. Five stages of grief. Don't answer the door.

A first-person horror game built in Unreal Engine 5.
