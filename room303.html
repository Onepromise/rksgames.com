ROOM 303

The story

CHECK IN.
NEVER CHECK OUT.

John, 31, an American artist, travels to Tokyo chasing inspiration for his next game. Lost in the neon maze of Kabukicho, he finds a hotel that seems to have been expecting him.

The key reads 303. The receptionist's smile doesn't reach his eyes. And in 2011, three guests checked into this hotel — none of them ever left.

Alone in a city that never sleeps, with only a dying phone for company, John must uncover what happened in room 303… before the hotel decides he's staying too.

Your phone is your only weapon. Its camera reveals what the naked eye can't see. Its flashlight keeps the dark at bay. And its messages aren't all from the living.
